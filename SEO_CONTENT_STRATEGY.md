# MittPsyke SEO Content Strategy
## Building Topical Authority for Swedish Mental Health

---

## 📋 Overview

This strategy builds topical authority around 8 core mental health topics with a calm, supportive, non-clinical tone. The structure uses:
- **8 Pillar Pages** (comprehensive core topics)
- **60-80 Cluster Articles** (5-10 articles per pillar)
- **15 Practical Tools Pages** (high-conversion standalone)

---

## 🏛️ TIER 1: PILLAR PAGES

### URL Hierarchy
```
mittpsyke.se/guides/[topic]/
```

### 1️⃣ ÅNGEST – En komplett guide
**URL:** `mittpsyke.se/guides/angest/`
**Meta Title:** Ångest – Vad det är, symptom & vad som hjälper | MittPsyke
**Meta Description:** En lugn guide till ångest. Läs om symptom, orsaker, vad som hjälper och självhjälpsövningar för att hantera ångest i vardagen.
**Word Count:** 3500-4000 words

#### Content Structure:
```markdown
# Ångest – En komplett guide

[Hero section with warm, relatable image]

## Vad är ångest?
- Clear, non-clinical definition
- Distinguish from normal worry
- Normalize the experience
- ~300 words

## Hur känns ångest?
- Physical sensations (heart racing, sweating, breathing)
- Thoughts (catastrophic thinking, "what if")
- Emotions
- Behaviors (avoidance, checking)
- ~400 words

## Symtom på ångest
- Fysiska symptom
- Kognitiva symptom
- Emotionella symptom
- Beteenderelaterade symptom
- ~350 words

## Varför uppstår ångest?
- Biologiska faktorer (explained simply)
- Life circumstances
- Stress and burnout
- Past experiences
- Avoidance patterns maintain anxiety
- ~400 words

## Varför börjar ångest ofta on kvällen?
- Biological rhythms
- Less distractions = more focus on fears
- Cortisol and circadian rhythms
- ~200 words

## Är ångest farligt?
- Reassurance (without toxic positivity)
- Physical safety
- Long-term impacts of avoidance
- ~300 words

## Vad som hjälper vid ångest
- Exposure and gradual approach
- Breathing techniques
- Acceptance and mindfulness
- Lifestyle factors
- Professional support
- ~500 words

## Självhjälpsövningar för ångest
- 5 Breathing techniques (with steps)
- Progressive muscle relaxation
- Grounding technique (5-4-3-2-1)
- Cognitive reframing
- ~600 words

## När bör man söka professionell hjälp?
- How to know it's time
- What therapy options exist
- Where to find support in Sweden
- ~250 words

## Relaterat innehål
[Links to 8-10 cluster articles]

## CTA
**Reflektera kring ångest i MittPsyke**
"I MittPsyke kan du i din egen takt utforska dina tankar och känslor kring ångest. En lugn, tryggt miljö för självreflexion."
[Button: "Börja reflektera"]
```

## 🔗 INTERNAL LINKING STRATEGY

### Hub & Spoke Model

**Hub (Pillar):** Each pillar page links TO all cluster articles
**Spokes (Clusters):** Each cluster article links BACK to main pillar
**Tools:** Each tool links to relevant pillar + 2-3 cluster articles

### Example: Ångest Cluster

```
mittpsyke.se/guides/angest/ (HUB)
├─ Links to: (in a "Läs mer i serien" section)
│  ├─ Vad händer i kroppen vid ångest
│  ├─ Är ångest farligt?
│  ├─ 5 Andningsövningar mot ångest
│  ├─ Skillnaden mellan oro och ångest
│  └─ ... (all 8 articles)
│
└─ Links to related tools:
   ├─ Grounding 5-4-3-2-1
   ├─ 4-7-8 andning
   └─ Body Scan meditation

Each cluster article links:
├─ Back to main pillar (Ångest – En komplett guide)
├─ To 2-3 related cluster articles
└─ To 1-2 relevant tools
```

### Cross-Pillar Linking

**Ångest → Stress & Utmattning**
- "Chronic anxiety often leads to burnout" → Link to stress pillar

**Övertänkande → Depression**
- "Rumination is common in depression" → Link to depression pillar

**Social ångest → Relationsproblem**
- "Social anxiety affects relationships" → Link to relations pillar

**Självkänsla → Depression**
- "Poor self-esteem is a symptom of depression" → Link to depression pillar

## 🎯 TONE GUIDELINES FOR ALL CONTENT

### DO:
✅ Use "you/your" language (intimate, relatable)
✅ Normalize experiences ("Many people feel...")
✅ Validate emotions without dramatizing
✅ Offer practical tools, not just theory
✅ Include caveats ("This isn't a substitute for professional help")
✅ Use analogies to explain complex concepts
✅ Break up text with subheadings and lists
✅ Acknowledge complexity without overwhelming

### DON'T:
❌ Use clinical/medical jargon without explaining
❌ Oversimplify ("Just think positive")
❌ Make absolute promises ("This will fix you")
❌ Shame or judge
❌ Focus on diagnosis  (focus on experience)
❌ Create panic ("You must seek help immediately")
❌ Create dependency on MittPsyke ("Only we can help")
❌ Use overly cheerful tone in serious topics

### Tone Examples:

**❌ Clinical:**
"The amygdala's hyperactivation leads to generalized anxiety disorder."

**✅ Accessible:**
"When your amygdala (your brain's alarm system) becomes overactive, it can trigger anxiety in situations that aren't actually dangerous."

**❌ Dismissive:**
"Anxiety is just in your head."

**✅ Validating & Honest:**
"Anxiety might be triggered by thoughts, but the physical sensations you feel are completely real. Your body is responding the way it's designed to."
